# Acme Corp Employee Handbook

## Version 3.2 | Last Updated: Q1 2026

---

## 1. Company Overview

**Acme Corp** is a technology company founded in 2010 and headquartered in Austin, Texas. We build cloud-native data infrastructure products used by over 4,000 enterprise clients worldwide.

Our mission: *"To make data accessible, trustworthy, and actionable for every organization."*

---

## 2. Work Schedule and Hours

### Standard Hours
- Core hours: **9:00 AM – 5:00 PM local time**, Monday through Friday.
- Engineering teams may adopt **flexible hours** with manager approval.
- Remote employees must maintain a minimum 4-hour overlap with Austin Core Hours (Central Time).

### Overtime Policy
Non-exempt employees receive 1.5× base pay for hours exceeding 40 per week. Exempt employees are not eligible for overtime pay but may be granted **comp time** at manager discretion.

---

## 3. Paid Time Off (PTO)

| Employment Level | Annual PTO (days) |
|---|---|
| Individual Contributors (L1–L4) | 18 |
| Senior / Staff (L5–L6) | 22 |
| Principal / Distinguished (L7+) | 25 |
| New hire during first 6 months | Accrual only (1.5 days/month) |

### Holidays
Acme Corp observes **12 federal holidays** plus **2 floating holidays** per year. Employees may carry over a maximum of **5 unused PTO days** to the following year; any balance beyond 5 days is forfeited at December 31.

### Sick Leave
All employees receive **10 paid sick days per year**, separate from PTO. Sick leave does not carry over.

---

## 4. Benefits

### Health Insurance
Acme Corp covers **90% of the premium** for individual medical coverage and **75%** for dependent coverage. Plans available: Acme Gold PPO, Acme Silver HMO, and HealthShare HSA-Eligible Plan.

The **open enrollment period** runs from **November 1 – November 30** each year. Employees hired mid-year have a 30-day window from their start date.

### Dental and Vision
- Dental: 100% covered for preventive care; 80% basic; 50% major.
- Vision: One exam per year plus up to $200 for frames/lenses fully covered.

### 401(k) Retirement Plan
Acme Corp matches **100% of employee contributions up to 4% of salary**. Vesting is immediate on the match. The 401(k) provider is **Fidelity Investments**.

### Equity (RSUs)
All full-time employees at L3 and above receive **RSU grants** vesting over a **4-year cliff-plus-monthly schedule**: 25% vests at the 1-year cliff, then monthly thereafter.

## 5. Code of Conduct

All employees must review and sign the Code of Conduct annually. Key provisions:

- **Conflicts of Interest**: Employees must disclose any outside employment or financial interest in a competing company to HR within 14 days.
- **Confidentiality**: Proprietary information (source code, customer lists, pricing) must never be shared outside Acme Corp without written authorization from the Legal team.
- **Respectful Workplace**: Zero tolerance for harassment, discrimination, or retaliation. Reports go to hr@acmecorp.example.com or the anonymous **EthicsLine** at 1-800-555-ETHICS.

---

## 6. Remote Work Policy

Acme Corp is a **hybrid-first company**. Engineering, Product, and Design teams may work remotely full-time. Sales, Customer Success, and Operations roles require in-office presence at least **2 days per week**.

### Equipment
Remote employees receive a **$1,500 one-time home office stipend** and a company-issued MacBook Pro (M-series) or equivalent Windows laptop upon hire. Equipment must be returned within 5 business days of termination.

### Internet Reimbursement
Remote employees are reimbursed up to **$60/month** for home internet access. Submit via the **Concur expense portal** by the last day of the month.

---

## 7. Performance Reviews

Acme Corp runs a **bi-annual review cycle**:
- **Mid-year calibration**: July (growth check-in, no comp changes).
- **Annual review**: January (compensation adjustments and promotion decisions).

**Rating Scale** (1–5):
1. Below Expectations
2. Approaching Expectations
3. Meets Expectations
4. Exceeds Expectations
5. Outstanding

An overall rating of **4 or 5** makes an employee **eligible for promotion** in the same cycle. Consecutive ratings of **1 or 2** may initiate a **Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)**.

---

## 8. Learning & Development

- **Acme Academy**: Internal LMS with 200+ courses.
- **External Learning Budget**: $2,000 per employee per year (conferences, certifications, books).
- **Study Leave**: Employees may take up to 5 days of paid study leave per year for approved professional development.
- **Tuition Reimbursement**: Up to $5,250/year for accredited degree programs (must remain employed for 12 months after reimbursement or repay proportionally).

---

## 9. Parental Leave

- **Primary caregiver**: 16 weeks fully paid.
- **Secondary caregiver**: 6 weeks fully paid.
- Applies to birth, adoption, and foster placement.
- Must be taken within **12 months of the qualifying event**.

---

## 10. Separation & Offboarding

### Voluntary Resignation
Two weeks written notice is expected. Final paycheck issued on the next regular pay date following the last day worked (per applicable state law).

### Involuntary Termination
Employees terminated without cause receive severance: **2 weeks pay per year of service**, up to a maximum of **26 weeks**. Benefits continue through the last day of the termination month.
