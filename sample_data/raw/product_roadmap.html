Acme DataStream Product Roadmap — 2026

Confidential — Internal Only (Tier 3)

Last updated: January 15, 2026 | Owner: Product Management

Strategic Themes for 2026

  1. Real-Time Intelligence: Move from data delivery to actionable insights at the edge.
  2. Enterprise Compliance: SOC 2 Type II re-certification, HIPAA readiness, and EU Data Residency.
  3. Developer Experience: Cut time-to-first-event from 45 minutes to under 5 minutes.
  4. Platform Extensibility: Open connector marketplace and transformation SDK.

Q1 2026 (January – March)

GA Releases

  • Stream Schemas v2: JSON Schema validation for all stream records. Backward-compatible with v1 streams.
  • Multi-Region Active-Active: Streams replicated across US-East and EU-West with < 100ms cross-region lag.
  • Connector: Salesforce CRM: Change-data-capture for Salesforce objects. Available on Pro and Enterprise tiers.

Beta Releases

  • Acme AI Anomaly Detection: ML-based anomaly alerts on stream metrics. Beta opt-in for Enterprise customers.

Deprecations

  • API v1: Officially deprecated Q1 2026. End-of-life: December 31, 2026. Migration guide: docs.acmecorp.example.com/migrate-v1-v2.
  • HTTP/1.1 for record publishing: HTTP/2 only from Q1 2026. Clients using HTTP/1.1 will receive a deprecation header.

Q2 2026 (April – June)

Planned GA

  • HIPAA Business Associate Agreement (BAA): Healthcare customers can process PHI on Acme DataStream once BAA is signed. Available on Enterprise only.
  • EU Data Residency: Customer data never leaves the EU-West region when the EU Residency flag is set on the organization.
  • Connector Marketplace (v1): Third-party developers can publish connectors. Initial launch with 5 certified partners.
  • CLI v2: Acme CLI rewritten in Go. 10× faster cold start. Supports all API v2 operations.

Planned Beta

  • Stream Joins: Join two streams on a key within a time window. SQL-like syntax. Beta with design partners.
  • Transformation SDK (Python): Write custom transformations in Python with local testing support.

Q3 2026 (July – September)

Planned GA

  • Stream Joins GA: Exit beta; available on Pro and Enterprise.
  • SOC 2 Type II Renewal: Annual audit scheduled for August 2026. Report available to Enterprise customers under NDA.
  • Real-Time Dashboard Builder: Drag-and-drop dashboards powered by stream data. No-code, embeddable.
  • Acme AI Anomaly Detection GA: Production-ready after Q1 beta feedback. Available on Pro and Enterprise.

Planned Beta

  • Acme DataSync: Bi-directional sync between two Acme streams with exactly-once delivery semantics.
  • GraphQL API: GraphQL endpoint alongside existing REST API. Read-only initially.

Q4 2026 (October – December)

Planned GA

  • GraphQL API GA: Full read-write GraphQL support.
  • Transformation SDK GA: Python and TypeScript SDKs with operator certification program.
  • Free Tier Expansion: Increase Free Tier record limit from 1M to 5M/month to accelerate bottom-up adoption.

Research / Exploratory

  • Edge Streaming Nodes: Run lightweight ingest nodes at the edge (Cloudflare Workers). Feasibility study ongoing.
  • Acme DataLake Integration: Native export to Apache Iceberg format in customer-owned S3 buckets.

Key Success Metrics for 2026

  • Grow Enterprise ARR by 40% year-over-year.
  • Achieve NPS of 55+ by Q4 (current: 48).
  • Time-to-first-event: reduce from 45 minutes to 5 minutes.
  • Connector Marketplace: 20 certified connectors by EOY.
  • Developer signups: 50,000 new developer accounts in 2026.

Dependencies and Risks

  • Multi-Region: Dependent on AWS EU-West capacity allocation. Potential delay if AWS TLA capacity is constrained.
  • HIPAA BAA: Legal review may take 6–8 weeks longer than planned. Q2 GA could slip to Q3.
  • Stream Joins: Core engine rewrite required. Engineering estimates carry 30% uncertainty.

Roadmap items marked "Planned" are subject to change. GA dates are targets, not commitments. Do not share externally without PM approval.
