Week 1: Orientation and Setup

Day 1 Checklist

  • Pick up your MacBook Pro from IT (Room 201, Austin HQ) or confirm shipping address for remote employees.
  • Activate your Acme Corp email at firstname.lastname@acmecorp.example.com.
  • Enroll in Okta SSO and set up a FIDO2 hardware key (YubiKey 5 provided by IT) as your primary MFA factor.
  • Join Slack: download the app and sign in with your Acme Corp email. Join #general, #engineering, and your team channel.
  • Complete mandatory Day 1 training modules in Acme Academy:
    1. Information Security Awareness (30 min)
    2. Code of Conduct (20 min)
    3. Privacy and Data Handling (25 min)

Week 2: Team Integration

Your Buddy Program

Every new hire is paired with an Onboarding Buddy for 90 days. Your buddy is a peer (not your manager) who helps you navigate Acme Corp culture, answers "silly" questions, and introduces you to the broader team.

Schedule a 30-minute coffee chat with your buddy in Week 1. Your buddy's name will be in your onboarding Jira ticket.

Key Meetings to Attend

  • Engineering All-Hands: Every other Wednesday, 10:00–11:00 AM CT. Company-wide engineering updates.
  • Team Stand-up: Daily, 9:15 AM CT (or team's preferred time). 15 minutes max.
  • Sprint Planning: Every 2 weeks (Monday of sprint start). Your manager will add you to the calendar invite.
  • 1:1 with Manager: Weekly, 30 minutes. First meeting should happen in Week 1.

Month 1: Ramp Up

Development Environment Setup

Follow the instructions in the README.md at the root of each repository. The main platform repo is at github.com/acmecorp/platform. Setup takes roughly 45–60 minutes for a fresh machine.

Prerequisites installed via the Acme Dev Bootstrap script:

  • Homebrew, Docker Desktop, Python 3.12+, Node.js 20 LTS, Go 1.22+
  • kubectl, Helm, Terraform CLI
  • AWS CLI v2 (configured via SSO, not static keys)

Your First PR

By the end of Month 1, you should have opened at least one pull request. Common starter tasks are labeled "good-first-issue" in GitHub. Talk to your manager if you don't have a ticket by Day 14.

Expense Reimbursement

Submit all reimbursable expenses via Concur within 30 days of incurring the expense. Late submissions (after 90 days) are not reimbursed.

Pre-approval is required for any single expense over $500. Send approval requests to your manager and cc finance@acmecorp.example.com.

Corporate credit cards (Brex) are issued to employees at L5 and above, and to anyone in Sales or Customer Success.

Payroll

Acme Corp runs payroll on a semi-monthly schedule: the 15th and last business day of each month. Direct deposit takes 1–2 business days to settle.

Payroll provider: ADP. You will receive an email from ADP to set up direct deposit in your first week.
